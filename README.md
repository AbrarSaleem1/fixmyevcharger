# FixMyEV Charger (evchargerrepair.us)

24/7 Nationwide Emergency EV Charger Repair, Tesla Wall Connector Diagnostics, Level 2 EVSE Service, and Commercial Charging Solutions.

## Features
- **Next.js App Router** with modern SSR and Turbopack optimization
- **Dynamic Nationwide Routing**: Dedicated landing pages for all 50 US States and thousands of cities (/[state]/[city]/)
- **6 Core EV Services**:
  - Tesla Wall Connector Repair
  - Level 2 EV Charger Repair
  - EV Charger Circuit & Breaker Repair
  - Emergency EV Charger Repair
  - DC Fast Charger Repair
  - Commercial EV Charger Repair
- **Rich Schema.org Structured Data**: LocalBusiness, Electrician, FAQPage, and BreadcrumbList schemas
- **Optimized for Cloudflare Pages / Workers** with OpenNext
